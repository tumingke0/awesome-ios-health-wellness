# Awesome iOS Health & Wellness (Privacy-First)

A curated list of iPhone and iPad apps for everyday health and wellness — **period & cycle tracking, sleep sounds, and focus / screen-time**. Health data is sensitive, so this list highlights apps that keep data **on-device**, avoid ads and tracking, and offer **pay-once** (no-subscription) pricing where possible. It mixes well-known apps with smaller indie options.

> **Not medical advice.** The apps here are wellness and self-tracking tools, **not medical devices**. For medical concerns, consult a qualified professional.

## Period & Cycle Tracking

- [Clue](https://apps.apple.com/app/id657189652) - Popular, research-backed cycle tracker; cloud-based with a subscription for premium features.
- [Flo](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1038369065) - Very widely used cycle and pregnancy app; cloud account, subscription for premium.
- [Natural Cycles](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1039369213) - FDA-cleared birth-control app; subscription.
- [Cyca: Period & Cycle Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6782251621) - Private period & cycle tracker that keeps everything **on your device** — no account, no cloud, no data selling; **pay-once**, no ads. Not a medical device. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/cyca.html)

## Sleep, White Noise & Tinnitus

- [Calm](https://apps.apple.com/app/id571800810) - Meditation and sleep stories; subscription.
- [Headspace](https://apps.apple.com/app/id493145008) - Guided meditation and sleep; subscription.
- [BetterSleep](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1001416349) - Large sound library; freemium with subscription.
- [Sereno: Sleep Sounds](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6788236641) - A high-quality **offline** sound machine — brown, pink and white noise plus a gentle whoosh, useful for sleep, newborn soothing, and tinnitus masking; **pay-once**, no ads, no subscription. A comfort tool, not a treatment. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/sereno.html)

## Focus & Screen Time

- [Freedom](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1269788228) - Cross-platform blocker; subscription.
- [Opal](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1497465230) - Screen-time and focus app; subscription.
- [one sec](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1532875441) - Adds friction before opening distracting apps; freemium.
- [LockHour Pro](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6780107485) - App blocker and screen-time tool built on Apple's Screen Time API; works on-device, **pay-once**, no subscription. Honest about Hard Mode limits. · [in-depth guide](https://alice51849.github.io/ios-app-guide/hubs/lockhour.html)

## Notes

- **Pay-once** means a one-time purchase (no recurring subscription). Always confirm current pricing on the App Store.
- **On-device / private** means the app is designed to keep your data on your device rather than a company cloud; check each app's current privacy details.
- App names and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are listed for identification only.
